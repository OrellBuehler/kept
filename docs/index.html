How it works

Download. Drop. Done.

No bank credentials, no screen scraping, no aggregator in the middle. Just the files your e-banking already gives you.

Export from your bank

Download a camt.053 statement or a CSV/Excel export from your e-banking. eBill invoices? Save the PDF.

Drop it into Kept

Preview every row before it lands. Kept warns when a file's opening balance doesn't continue your history.

Let it reconcile

Rules categorize, bills match to payments, budgets fill up, and the dashboard shows where you stand.

Privacy by architecture

Your finances never leave your server.

  • SQLite by default, PostgreSQL optional. Everything in a single database file you can copy, inspect and back up, or in your own PostgreSQL 17+ server.
  • Backups built in. On SQLite, download a consistent snapshot any time, or let Kept write one every day.
  • Every user is walled off. Local accounts with argon2id passwords; nobody sees anyone else's data.
  • Secrets encrypted at rest. Integration tokens are encrypted with a key only you hold.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does Kept connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. Kept never asks for your e-banking credentials. You download statements yourself and import them — camt.053, CSV or Excel. Re-importing overlapping files is safe.

Can I receive eBill invoices?

A self-hosted app can't receive eBill directly. Download the invoice PDF in your e-banking and upload it as a bill — Kept reads the QR data from it.

Does it handle multiple currencies?

Yes. Every amount keeps its currency, stored as exact integer minor units. Totals and net worth are shown per currency; Kept never converts them with a made-up exchange rate.

Do I need Paperless-ngx?

No. Kept stores bill PDFs itself. If you already run Paperless-ngx (2.16+), the optional integration pulls bills from it and writes their status back.

Can my household use it?

The administrator creates further local users. Each user has their own accounts, bills and budgets and only sees their own data.
