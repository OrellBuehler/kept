Support

Where to ask, and answers to the problems people run into first.

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/OrellBuehler/kept/issues. Say which phone and system version you use, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), the Kept version your server runs, and what you did before the problem appeared. Issues are public, so leave out real account numbers, amounts and names, and blur screenshots.

Where to find answers

  • The Kept documentation covers installing and running the server.
  • The apps' readme explains what the Android and iPhone apps do, how sign-in and reminders work, and how they are built and released.
  • The issue tracker is the place for questions and bug reports. Search it first, your question may already have an answer.
  • The releases page has the Android APK of the releases that include the apps.
  • The TestFlight beta has the iPhone app. Install Apple's TestFlight app first, then open the link on the phone.

Connecting and signing in

"No Kept server answers at this address"

The app could not find a Kept server where you pointed it. Check:

  • The address. Type it as you would in a browser, for example kept.example.org. Without a prefix the app uses https. For a server in your home network use the full address, such as http://192.168.1.5:3000.
  • https or http. If the server only speaks http, say so in the address. If it speaks https, do not add http://.
  • The version. The server must be Kept 0.6 or newer, the first version with the app interface.
  • The reverse proxy. If one sits in front of Kept, it must pass /api/app/v1 through to the server, not only the pages of the website.
  • The network. Your phone has to reach the server: a home address works only at home or over a VPN.
The app warns that the connection is not encrypted

You entered an address starting with http://. That is fine inside a home network you trust, but anyone on the network path could read what is sent, including your password. Put Kept behind https and use that address instead.

I cannot sign in with a passkey

Choose "Sign in with the browser" on the sign-in screen. The app opens your server's own login page, where your passkey and your password manager work as on the web, and signs you in when you confirm there. This needs Kept 0.7 or newer on the server. The app's own form takes a password and the code from your authenticator app; with an older server and an account that has only passkeys, use a recovery code there. You can create an authenticator and recovery codes on the website under Settings, Security.

I was signed out unexpectedly

A session ends when:

  • you change your password on the web, or sign out there;
  • it has not been used for 30 days (every use extends it);
  • an administrator ends it;
  • you restore a phone from a backup or move to a new one. The session is kept on the device only, so sign in again.

Signing in again does not lose anything: your data is on the server.

Why does a page open in the browser?

Security settings (two-step verification and passkeys), API tokens, Paperless and the administrator pages are not in the apps. They open on your server's website in the browser, where you sign in as usual.

Bills and files

A photo of a bill is refused as too large

Kept stores a bill as a PDF of at most 20 MB. A photo or scan is turned into a PDF on the phone, and the app tries smaller versions of it before it gives up. If it is still refused, take the photo again from a little further away or in a lower resolution (on iPhone you can also use the scanner). A PDF you pick from your files is not resized: shrink it first.

Reminders

Reminders do not arrive
  1. Switch them on in the app under Settings, Reminders, and choose what to be reminded about.
  2. Allow notifications for Kept in the system settings. The app tells you when they are switched off.
  3. Know that there is no push service. The app asks your server in the background, roughly every six hours, and only when the system allows it. Battery optimisation on Android, and Low Power Mode or switched-off Background App Refresh on iPhone, can hold the check back for a long time. Exempt Kept from battery optimisation if reminders are late.
  4. Open the app: it checks every time it starts. Reminders for a bill's due date are scheduled ahead, so they arrive even while the server cannot be reached.

Your data on the phone

How do I remove everything from the device?

In Settings choose Disconnect from this server. It signs you out, removes the server address and your reminder settings and cancels scheduled reminders. A document you opened (kept in the app's cache until the system clears it) stays until you delete the app, or clear its storage in the system settings on Android. Nothing is deleted on the server.

What the apps store is listed in the privacy policy.

Security problems

If you found a security problem in Kept, please do not open a public issue. Report it privately with GitHub's private vulnerability report for the repository.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub. Kept is open source, so you can also read how it works in the repository.
